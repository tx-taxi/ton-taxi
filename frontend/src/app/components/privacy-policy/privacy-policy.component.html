Privacy Policy

Audience measurement

tx.taxi uses Cloudflare Web Analytics for page views and performance, and a first-party daily cookie shared across its explorers to count browsers once per UTC day. The daily audience collector stores only a daily identifier, the explorer hostname, and whether there was interaction. It does not receive search text, addresses, transaction IDs, URLs, or IP addresses. Individual daily records expire after 31 days; aggregate counts are retained for 400 days. Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control disable this first-party measurement. Details and opt-out.

Public blockchain lookups

TON addresses, transaction hashes, and block identifiers submitted to the explorer are public blockchain data. Requests are processed by ton.tx.taxi and its configured public TON data providers.

Local preferences

Display preferences such as language, timezone, fiat currency, amount mode, and theme may be stored in your browser. These preferences are used to render the interface and are not TON account credentials.

Operational records

The hosting stack and upstream data providers may retain routine security and request logs. Do not use a public explorer when a lookup itself is sensitive; use your own TON node and local tooling instead.

External links

Links to TON documentation, Telegram, u.software, TONAPI, and the upstream Mempool project are governed by those services' own privacy policies.
