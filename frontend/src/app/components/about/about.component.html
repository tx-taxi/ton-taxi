About ton.tx.taxi

Explore TON blocks, messages, transactions, accounts, jettons, and NFTs in the familiar tx.taxi interface.

Follow the whole transaction

TON activity can span several contracts and messages. Follow each step, inspect its status and fees, and open the full transaction details.

See what a wallet holds

Browse jettons and collectibles, including on-chain Telegram gifts, usernames, numbers, and TON domains. Open an item for ownership, collection, attributes, and transfer history.

Built on open source
